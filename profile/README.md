## 🌟 What is AWS Cloud Club WildQuacc?
**AWS Cloud Club - WildQuacc** is a student-led organization under the global **AWS Cloud Clubs** initiative by Amazon Web Services. We provide students with hands-on exposure to cloud computing and AWS technologies through peer-led learning, workshops, projects, and industry-aligned events.

Our mission is to empower students to apply cloud concepts through real-world use cases in:
* 🛡️ **Cloud Technology & Security**
* 🤖 **Artificial Intelligence (GenAI)**
* 📊 **Data Analytics**

---

## 🎯 Our Core Mission
We foster a culture of **curiosity, collaboration, and continuous learning**. Through hands-on experiences, we aim to:
- ✅ Strengthen technical competence in cloud-native development.
- ✅ Support academic growth through industry-aligned workshops.
- ✅ Prepare members for future careers in an evolving technology landscape.
